# Real User Testing Protocol

**Role:** Research Lead  
**Prerequisite:** Hero Frozen — [`HERO_FREEZE.md`](./HERO_FREEZE.md)  
**Participants:** 5 people who have actually done a hackathon  
**Method:** Observe. Do not interview.

---

## Rules (be boring)

- Do **not** explain the product  
- Do **not** rescue them  
- Do **not** defend design  
- Do **not** say “Actually…”  
- Every explanation destroys the experiment  

Hand them the phone. Say almost nothing.

---

## Capture per participant (behavior, not opinions)

### 1. First sentence
Before touching anything:

> “I think this app…”

→ Positioning.

### 2. First hesitation
Where do they stop?

→ Biggest UX problem.

### 3. Biggest smile
When do they react?

→ Emotional high point.

### 4. Biggest confusion
Where do they become uncertain (not annoyed)?

### 5. Final answer
Ask **only**:

> **Would this have changed which team you joined?**

---

## Success criteria (out of 5)

| Target | Signal |
|---|---|
| **4/5** | Immediately understand: helps decide which team to join |
| **4/5** | Naturally pause at *You’ll have three captains and no ship* (no prompting) |
| **3/5** | Lived recognition: “That’s actually true” / “Yeah…” / “I’ve seen that” |
| **3/5** | Would listen to the recommendation |

---

## Ignore

- “Looks nice”  
- “Cool UI”  
- “Nice colors”  

Those do not validate the product.

---

## Gold moment

Laugh → quiet →

> “Yeah… that’s exactly how our last hackathon went.”

Worth more than twenty UI compliments.

---

## Participant priority (if five ideal hackers are hard)

Be explicit about who each person is when reporting.

1. Students who have participated in hackathons *(ideal)*  
2. Students currently preparing for hackathons  
3. Technical students who have worked in project teams  

Do not delay indefinitely waiting for a perfect cohort.

---

Do **not** report “liked it / cool / nice UI.”


---

## Decision rule (Research Interpreter)

Every proposed change must satisfy:

> **Observed behavior from multiple users**, or a reproducible defect.

Not preference. Not aesthetics. Not “I have an idea.” Only evidence.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| **A** — Understand, trust, would change decision | **Ship.** Do not “improve” validated behavior. |
| **B** — Same hesitation / misunderstanding across users | **Fix only that behavior**, then validate again. |

Everything else stays frozen.

### Interpreter questions (when results return)

1. What behavior repeated?  
2. What behavior surprised us?  
3. Which behaviors materially affect trust or decision?  
4. Which observations are noise and should be ignored?  

Then: change nothing · change one thing · or ship.

Protect validated work. Only change what reality disproves.
