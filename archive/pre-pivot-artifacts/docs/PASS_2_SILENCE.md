# Pass 2 — Silence (Cinema)

**Status:** Active — awaiting muted take  
**Pass 1:** 🟢 Accepted — sentence as artwork  
**Reviewer:** Judge #27 (cinema, not UI)

This is **not** UI. Not animation polish. Not spacing. Not typography.

When a muted recording is brought to review, evaluation is **frame by frame** using only the criteria below.

---

## The only question

> **Did the silence transform a clever sentence into an unforgettable moment?**

If yes → move on.  
If not → keep **cutting**, not adding, until it does.

Out of scope for this pass: fonts, spacing, implementation, code, React, animations.

---

## Emotional target

Not engagement. Not retention. Not conversion.

One emotion:

```text
...

Yeah.

That would happen.
```

## Frame-by-frame phases

### Phase 1 — Arrival (0–2s)

> Does the screen arrive quietly?

Feel like entering a room — not like a modal opened.

| Fail | Succeed |
|---|---|
| Motion still settling while reading | Frame is already calm |
| Layout shifting | Eye does not hunt |
| Elements fading independently | Quiet arrival |

### Phase 2 — First Read (2–6s)

Eye lands here immediately:

```text
You'll have three
captains
and no ship.
```

Nothing else competes.

> Did my eye ever leave the sentence?

If yes → the frame failed.

### Phase 3 — Silence (6–14s)

**This is the entire review.**

Nothing happens.

No hint. No pulse. No shimmer. No motion. No CTA animation.

The sentence must survive its own silence.

> Am I still thinking about the sentence?  
> — or —  
> Am I waiting for the next animation?

Those are opposite experiences. Only the first passes.

### Phase 4 — Decision to Tap

Most important moment.

Tap because:

> **"I need to know why."**

Not because:

> "The flow continues."

That distinction = whether curiosity exists.

---

## Timing nuance

Docs may say 12–14s. Agree as a floor for rehearsal.

**Do not time seconds in the final cut.**

Time **completion of thought.**

Sometimes 10s. Sometimes 16s.

Correct duration:

> The first moment when continuing feels inevitable.

Not earlier. Not later.

### Biggest failure mode

Pride in the sentence → linger after it has landed → the moment dies.

End the hold the instant **curiosity overtakes reflection**.

Instinct, not stopwatch.

---

## Three-watch protocol

### Watch 1 — Emotion
No pausing. Just feel.

### Watch 2 — Frame by frame
Eye movement only.

### Watch 3 — Memory
> If the Why button disappeared, would I still remember this scene tomorrow?

If yes → succeeded.

---

## Success criteria

Pass 2 is complete only when the reviewer forgets they are evaluating software.

It should feel like the reveal of a **documentary** — not the reveal of a feature.

---

## What we do NOT do

- Add entrance animations to manufacture the pause  
- Add timers / auto-advance / pulse on Why?  
- Redesign the accepted Pass 1 hero  
- Fill silence with captions or explanatory sound  

Cut until the silence works. Do not decorate the silence.
